User Data Deletion — WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? Last updated: October 26, 2026 WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? does not operate its own server or database to store players' game data. How to Delete Local Game Data The game may store gameplay progress or scores locally on your device. To remove this locally stored data: 1. Open the browser or game environment where you play. 2. Clear the relevant website or game storage using the available browser or platform settings. 3. Reopen the game. Any progress stored only on the device may have been reset. Please note that the exact steps depend on the device and platform used to access the game. Data Stored by Third-Party Platforms If Meta or another third-party service processes information independently, that information is subject to the relevant service's policies and controls. The developer cannot directly delete data held independently by those services. For questions about data deletion, contact: Questara Interactive Email: questarainteractive@gmail.com
