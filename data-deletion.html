User Data Deletion

WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This page explains how players can remove gameplay data associated with WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?, developed by Questara Interactive.

1. Data Stored by the Developer

The developer does not operate its own game server or database to store players' gameplay progress or scores. The game may store progress or scores locally on the player's device.

2. How to Delete Local Game Data

To remove locally stored game data, players can clear the relevant browser or game-environment storage using the available device or platform settings.

The exact steps depend on the device and platform used to access the game. Deleting local data may permanently reset saved progress or scores stored on that device.

3. Data Managed by Third-Party Platforms

Meta or other third-party services may process information independently under their own policies. The developer cannot directly delete information held independently by those services. Players should use the relevant platform's privacy settings or data-request process for such information.
