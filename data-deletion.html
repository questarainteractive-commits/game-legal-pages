WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?

User Data Deletion Instructions

Developer: Questara Interactive | Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Architecture & Data Storage Overview

WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? is developed by Questara Interactive. We prioritize user privacy and client-side efficiency. We do not maintain external developer servers or databases to store your player profile, scores, or gameplay statistics.

Your gameplay scores, round streak counters, settings, and local statistics are stored exclusively on your device within your local browser storage or the Facebook Instant Games environment.

2. How to Delete Your Local Game Data

Since your progress and statistics are saved locally on your device, you have full authority to delete this data at any time:

Important Notice: Deleting your local storage will permanently clear your high scores, streak records, and game settings. This action cannot be undone.

Option A: Reset via Game Settings (In-App)

  1. Open WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?.
  2. Click or tap the Settings / Quick Menu icon on the top right header.
  3. Select the Statistics tab.
  4. Click the Reset All Stats button to wipe your local records immediately.

Option B: Reset via Web Browser Settings

  1. Open your web browser's Settings or Preferences menu.
  2. Navigate to Privacy & Security > Site Settings or Clear Browsing Data.
  3. Locate the cached site data for the game domain and clear local storage / cache.

3. Facebook / Meta Platform Data Management

If you play WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? as a Facebook Instant Game, Meta Platforms, Inc. manages platform-level app connections through your Facebook Account settings. To remove WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? from your Facebook account:

  1. Log in to your Facebook Account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, click Apps and Websites.
  4. Find WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? under active or expired apps.
  5. Click Remove to disconnect the game from your Facebook profile and revoke permissions.

For more details on managing platform data stored by Meta, please visit the Facebook Help Center - Apps & Websites.
