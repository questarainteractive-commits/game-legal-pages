WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?

Privacy Policy

Developer: Questara Interactive | Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?, a social deduction game developed by Questara Interactive ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you access and play our game through Facebook Instant Games or web browsers.

We are committed to respecting player privacy. We designed our game to function locally without collecting or storing personal data on external developer servers.

2. Data Collection & Processing

Questara Interactive does not operate proprietary game servers or databases to store your personal information, scores, or gameplay progress.

When you play WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR?:

  • Local Data Storage: Game progress, player statistics, high scores, and settings are saved locally on your device or via the instant game environment storage provided by the host platform.
  • No Personal Profile Creation: We do not ask you to register a separate account with Questara Interactive, nor do we harvest email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

3. Platform Data & Facebook Services

While Questara Interactive does not independently collect or store your personal data, WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? operates on the Facebook Instant Games platform hosted by Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta").

Meta may collect and process information regarding your usage of Instant Games under their own privacy practices. This may include platform-level identifiers, basic gaming profile preferences, or device metrics necessary to load and render the game.

For detailed information on how Meta handles your data, please review Meta's official policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Facebook Terms of Service

4. Advertising and Analytics

At present, WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? does not display third-party advertisements or integrate external third-party analytics tracking tools.

If we decide to introduce third-party ad networks or analytics services in future game updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly to reflect the specific services used, the data collected by those providers, and instructions on how to manage your privacy choices.

5. Managing Your Local Data

Because your game progress and preferences are stored locally within your browser or game platform cache, you have direct control over this data:

  • You can reset or clear your game statistics and saved progress at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or application data.
  • Clearing local data will permanently reset your game records and preferences.

6. Children's Privacy

WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? is a general audience social deduction game. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. Since we do not operate external database servers, no personal data from players of any age is retained or stored by Questara Interactive.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in game features, legal requirements, or platform policies. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
