Last updated: October 26, 2026

WHO'S THE IMPOSTOR? is a game developed by Questara Interactive.

1. Information We Collect

The developer does not operate its own game server or database to collect or store players' gameplay data.

The game may store gameplay information, such as progress or scores, locally on the player's device using browser or game-environment storage. This information is not transmitted to a game server operated by the developer.

2. How Local Data Is Managed

Locally stored game data may be removed by clearing the relevant browser or game storage, where those controls are available. Removing this data may reset saved progress.

3. Third-Party Services

The game is designed for Facebook Instant Games and may rely on services provided by Meta. Data processed independently by Meta is subject to Meta's applicable privacy policies and terms.

If third-party advertising or analytics services are added, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant services and data practices.

4. Children's Privacy

The developer does not intentionally collect personal information through a game-operated server. The platform hosting the game may process information under its own policies. Parents and guardians should review the applicable platform policies and supervise children's use of online services.
